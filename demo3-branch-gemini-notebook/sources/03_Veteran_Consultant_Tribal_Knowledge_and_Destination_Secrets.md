## 1. Cruise Ship Cabin Traps & Deck Rules

### P&O *Arvia* & *Iona* (Excel-Class Ships)
* **THE DECK 8 LIFEBOAT OVERHANG TRAP (CRITICAL):**
  * Newer consultants frequently book **Deck 8 Balcony Cabins** because they are priced £80–£100 lower than Deck 12 and are listed as *"Unobstructed Sea View"*.
  * **Reality Check:** On both *Arvia* and *Iona*, the yellow lifeboats and heavy steel davit arms are suspended directly underneath Deck 8 along the Promenade roof. While you can look straight out at the horizon, **looking down at the water is completely blocked by the tops of the lifeboats**, and the Promenade deck walkway on Deck 8 lets passers-by look into forward/aft conservatory balconies!
  * **Veteran Fix:** Always upgrade couples (especially anniversary bookings like `CUST-101` on package `B629C`) to **Deck 11 or Deck 12 Mid-Ship (Cabins `C1202`–`C1240`)**. It provides a 100% clear vertical view straight down to the ocean, zero Promenade foot-traffic noise, and minimal motion in Atlantic swells between Madeira and Tenerife.
* **COELIAC & GLUTEN-FREE DINING ON P&O *ARVIA*:**
  * Do NOT wait until embarkation day to notify the ship of Coeliac Disease. Pre-log code `GF-MED-COELIAC` in the booking PNR at least 14 days before sailing AND book the first-night dinner at **The Olive Grove** (Deck 8 Mid-Ship), where the head chef prepares dedicated gluten-free Mediterranean sharing boards in a separate allergen-safe galley bay.
* **MOBILITY SCOOTER DOORWAY RULE (*IONA* & *ARVIA*):**
  * Standard Balcony and Conservatory Mini-Suite doorways measure **56 cm** wide. Any passenger bringing a mobility scooter (typically **60 cm–64 cm** wide) **MUST** be booked into a designated **Fully Accessible Cabin** (e.g., `E1108` on Deck 11 Mid-Ship, which has a **72 cm doorway**, automatic power-assisted door opener, and a 170 cm internal turning circle). Under SOLAS maritime fire regulations, scooters parked in passenger corridors will be confiscated at Southampton embarkation.

## 2. Spain Family Beach Reality Checks (Costa del Sol & Balearics)

### Why You Should NEVER Book Central Marbella for Families Wanting "Soft Flat Sand & Shallow Water"

  * **Reality Check:** Most beaches around central Marbella (`Playa de Venus`, `Playa del Faro`) and San Pedro have **coarse, dark, stony/pebbly shingle at the waterline** and a **steep underwater drop-off shelf** within 3 metres of shore, plus a **45-to-55-minute coach transfer** from Malaga Airport.
