## 2. Standard Operating Procedure: Eliminating the 10-Minute `iSell` Re-Keying Tax & 48-Hour Courtesy Follow-Up

1. **Automated `iSell` Field Mapping:**
   * Previously, consultants spent **8 to 10 minutes per booking** manually copying PNR references, flight numbers, times, cabin numbers, and gross/commission splits from supplier confirmation screens into `iSell`.
   * Using the **Hays Branch SMILE Companion** or **Gemini Notebook**, extract the structured `iSell` paste block (`Quote Ref`, `Supplier PNR`, `Outbound/Inbound Flight`, `Cabin/Room Code`, `SSR Medical Alerts`, `Gross Price`, `Vista Commission £`) in one step.
2. **Mandatory 48-Hour Post-Quote / Post-Booking Courtesy Follow-Up (`TSK-BR-101` .. `TSK-BR-103`):**
   * Every branch booking or held option requires a **48-Hour Courtesy Call or WhatsApp/Email Follow-Up**:
     * **For Confirmed Bookings (`QT-MAID-901` / `TSK-BR-101`):** Confirm that Sarah's Coeliac dining request (`GF-MED-COELIAC`) is visible on their MyP&O Cruise Personaliser, confirm the `Holiday Extras` Gatwick Meet & Greet + No1 Lounge (`£209.00`) attachment, and remind David about the **£410.00 Euro balance** already on his `Sunny Spend` card (`#SS-9941-EUR`).
     * **For Open Family Quotes (`QT-MAID-902` / `TSK-BR-102`):** Call Emma Miller before the Free Child Place expires to confirm the switch from Marbella to **Sol Principe Torremolinos (`FAM-TOR-402`)** with the 12-minute private transfer.
